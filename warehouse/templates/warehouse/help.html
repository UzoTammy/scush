Stores & Facilities tracks every rented facility, its rent renewal, and its yearly levy. Here's what each part does.

  1. 1
    Add a Facility

    Open Facilities, click Add, fill in the form, and submit.

  2. 2
    Update a Facility

    Open Facilities, click the facility's name to open its detail page, click Update, adjust the record, and submit.

  3. 3
    Pay Rent

    A facility whose rent has expired shows a Rent Due link in the "Rented Facilities & Due Dates" table on this section's home page — click it, confirm Pay Now in the pop-up, then fill in the Pay Rent form and submit.

    To pay rent for a facility that isn't yet overdue, open Rent and click Pay Rent to pick any facility directly.

  4. 4
    Update or Adjust Rent Paid

    Open Rent to see every rent payment made this year. Click a record's ID to open it, adjust as needed, and submit.

  5. 5
    Pay Levy

    Open Levy and click Pay Levy, fill in the form, and submit.

  6. 6
    Update a Paid Levy

    Open Levy, click the facility name on the levy record of interest, adjust as needed, and submit.
