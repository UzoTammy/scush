Equity Pool tracks the Employee Profit-Sharing scheme: each participant's one-time Initial Capital Allocation, their share of every year's Employee Profit Pool, and how much of their running balance has vested (permanently theirs) versus is still unvested (at risk until the 3-year clock completes). {% if is_hrd %} You're viewing this as an HRD user, so the admin sections below apply to you. {% else %} You're viewing this as a staff member — the "My Equity Pool" section below is the one that applies to you; the Admin sections are included for reference. {% endif %}

  1. 1
    Where to find Equity Pool
    • Staff & HR hub (/staff/home/) — the quick-nav bar at the top has an Equity Pool link (admin list). Only visible/useful to HRD users.
    • Your Info (/profile/) — if you're an enrolled participant, a My Equity Pool button appears next to "Change Password". Not shown at all if you're not enrolled.
    • An employee's detail page — the HR Actions dropdown shows Equity Pool (if already a participant) or Add to Equity Pool (if eligible but not yet added). The stat-card row also shows an Equity Pool tile with that person's current vested balance.
    • From the list page — every participant's Staff ID / name links straight into their Participant Detail page.
  2. 2
    My Equity Pool (staff view)

    URL: /staff/my-equity/ — read-only, shows only your own data. What you'll see:

    • Current Balance — everything credited to you so far (initial allocation + every profit-pool credit), before subtracting anything forfeited or paid out.
    • Vested (protected) — the portion that's permanently yours regardless of what happens next.
    • Unvested (at risk) — the portion still subject to the vesting clock; this is what could be forfeited if you leave before it matures.
    • Vesting Status — either "Fully matured — safe" (once your 3-year clock completes) or a progress bar showing 0% → 33% → 66% → 100%.
    • My Ledger — every entry ever posted to your balance and when, in date order.

    If this page says you're "not currently enrolled," you're not a named participant in the scheme — nothing is wrong, there's just no Equity Pool data tied to your account.

  3. 3
    Equity Pool List (admin)

    URL: /staff/equity/list/ — HRD group only.

    • Total Pool Value stat — sum of every visible participant's current balance.
    • Zero-Balance Participants toggle — participants with a zero balance (e.g. fully paid-out, or added but not yet granted) are hidden by default. Click the tile to show/hide them. This only changes what's displayed — nothing is ever deleted; every ledger entry stays queryable from that person's detail page regardless.
    • The participants table shows Staff ID, Name, Role Code, this fiscal year's locked share %, Current/Vested/Unvested balance, Vesting %, and Status. Click any row to open that participant's detail page.

    Header buttons: Add Participant, Threshold Profit, Reallocate Shares, and Generate All Statements — each explained in its own section below.

  4. 4
    Posting a Profit Pool Credit

    On the list page, use the Post Profit Pool Credit form:

    1. Enter the Fiscal Year the credit belongs to (e.g. 2026).
    2. Enter the Total Pool Amount — the full Employee Profit Pool for that year (20% of NPAT above Threshold Profit), as one number.
    3. Click Split & Post to All Active Participants.

    Expected result: the system splits that total using each active participant's locked share allocation for the fiscal year you entered — not whatever their share happens to be today — and posts one ledger entry per participant. If a participant has no locked share for that year, they simply receive nothing from this posting (nothing errors).

  5. 5
    Adding a Participant

    URL: /staff/equity/add/

    1. Pick the staff member — the dropdown only lists actively employed staff who aren't already a participant. Management status doesn't matter; this section never reads or changes that flag.
    2. Enter their Role Code (e.g. GSM, SCM1) — this can differ from their job title/position, it's whatever code the MD supplied for the scheme.
    3. Enter their Initial Capital Allocation (NGN).
    4. Pick a Grant Date — there is deliberately no default here. This date starts their single vesting clock, so confirm it before saving.

    Expected result: saving creates the participant and immediately posts a grant ledger entry for the initial allocation. It does not give them a share of the current fiscal year's pool automatically — that only happens the next time you run Reallocate Shares, so adding someone mid-year doesn't disturb an already-locked year.

  6. 6
    Participant Detail & Ledger

    URL: /staff/employee/<id>/equity/

    • Four stat cards: Current Balance, Vested, Unvested, and Vesting % (with a "Fully Matured" badge once the 3-year clock completes).
    • Participant Info — role code, grant date, initial allocation, status.
    • Share Allocations by Fiscal Year — every year's share %, and whether it's Locked (permanent) or Pending (still editable via Reallocate Shares).
    • Ledger — the full append-only history: grants, profit-pool credits, clawbacks, forfeitures, payouts, each with date, fiscal year, amount, and note. Nothing here is ever edited or deleted — corrections are posted as new reversing entries.
  7. 7
    Clawback, Forfeiture & Separation

    These three action panels only appear on an active participant's detail page:

    • Post Clawback — for a net-loss fiscal year. Enter the amount and a required note (who approved it, and why). Expected result: the system refuses to save if the amount exceeds that participant's current unvested balance — vested amounts can never be clawed back, no matter what you enter.
    • Forfeiture (For Cause) — for termination due to fraud/misconduct. Requires an investigation reference/note. Expected result: zeroes the participant's entire balance — vested and unvested both — and marks them "Forfeited (Cause)".
    • Separation Payout — for resignation/layoff (not for cause). Expected result: if they're already fully matured, their whole balance is paid out. If not, only the vested portion is paid out and the unvested portion is posted as a forfeiture back to company reserves. Either way the participant is marked "Separated".

    All three ask for confirmation before submitting — they're one-way, ledger-writing actions, not something to undo by editing.

  8. 8
    Reallocating Shares

    URL: /staff/equity/reallocate/<fiscal_year>/ — e.g. open FY2027 to set up next year's shares.

    1. Opening the page for a new year pre-fills each active participant's row from last year's locked share, as a starting point.
    2. Edit the % for anyone whose share is changing this year.
    3. Save (don't lock) — stores your edits without finalizing them; you can come back and keep adjusting.
    4. Lock FY<year> — finalizes the year. Expected result: this is blocked with an error message unless the active participants' shares add up to exactly 100%. Once locked, that year's rows are frozen — a later reallocation for the next year never touches them.
  9. 9
    Threshold Profit

    URL: /staff/equity/threshold/ (list) and /staff/equity/threshold/add/ (new fiscal year).

    Each fiscal year needs a Threshold Profit record: the NPAT threshold above which the 20% Employee Profit Pool is calculated, the methodology behind that number, when it was approved, and when it was actually disclosed to staff.

    Expected result: if a year's Threshold Profit has been approved for more than 30 days and Disclosed Date is still blank, the list page shows a red warning banner — that's a live compliance breach, not a cosmetic reminder.

  10. 10
    Annual Allocation Statement (PDF)
    • Single — "Generate Statement (PDF)" on a participant's detail page. Uses the current fiscal year unless you pass a different one in the URL.
    • Bulk — "Generate All Statements (FY<year>)" on the list page produces one PDF per active participant for that year in one go.

    Expected result: each generated PDF is saved and listed on that participant's detail page, so you can re-download a past statement later without regenerating it. The PDF always carries the required non-entitlement disclaimer at the foot — it's informational, not a payment or a guarantee.

  11. 11
    Glossary
    Grant Date
    The date that starts a participant's single vesting clock. Admin-set, never defaulted or auto-filled.
    Vesting %
    0% before 12 months, 33% from 12–24 months, 66% from 24–36 months, 100% from 36 months onward.
    Fully Matured
    A permanent flag set once a participant crosses the 3-year mark. From then on, every new credit posts as vested immediately — the clock stops applying to them at all.
    Fiscal Year
    The year a share allocation or ledger entry belongs to (e.g. "2026"). Credits always use the share that was locked for their own fiscal year, even if a later reallocation changes that participant's share.
    Locked Share
    A fiscal year's share % becomes permanent once you lock it; only future years can be reallocated.
    Vested vs Unvested Balance
    Vested is permanently the participant's; Unvested is still subject to the clock and can be forfeited on early separation, or clawed back in a net-loss year (never below zero, never touching vested amounts).
  12. 12
    Common tasks, step by step

    A new manager just joined the scheme

    1. Equity Pool List → Add Participant.
    2. Fill in role code, initial allocation, and grant date → Save.
    3. They'll get a share once you next run Reallocate Shares for an upcoming fiscal year — not automatically for the current one.

    This year's profit pool needs to be paid out

    1. Confirm Threshold Profit is set (and disclosed) for the year.
    2. Equity Pool List → Post Profit Pool Credit → enter fiscal year + total amount → Post.
    3. Spot-check a participant's detail page ledger for the new Profit Pool Credit entry.

    Someone is resigning

    1. Open their Participant Detail page.
    2. Separation Payout → add a note if useful → confirm.
    3. Check their status changed to "Separated" and the ledger shows the payout (and forfeiture, if they weren't fully matured yet).

    Next year's shares need to change

    1. Equity Pool List → Reallocate Shares for the new fiscal year.
    2. Adjust the % values → Save (don't lock) while you're still finalizing.
    3. Once everything sums to 100%, click Lock.
