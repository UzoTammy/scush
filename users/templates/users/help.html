Invite User is the only way to create an account — no separate registration form. Here's the whole process, start to finish.

  1. 1
    Open Invite User

    Sidebar → Admin Tools → Invite User. Only Administrators and superusers can get in here.

  2. 2
    Pick the staff member

    Choose them from the list. Only active staff who don't already have an account show up — nothing to double-check.

  3. 3
    Copy the link

    A one-time link appears next to their name. Click the copy icon next to it.

  4. 4
    Send it to them

    However you'd normally reach them — email, chat, WhatsApp. It's good for 7 days.

  5. 5
    They open it — no login needed

    Their username is already decided for them, following firstname-staffid (e.g. john-07) — the same convention used everywhere in the app.

  6. 6
    They set a password — done

    They're signed in immediately, ready to use the app. Nothing left for you to do.

Good to know

Link statuses
Pending (still active), Expired (7 days passed, unused), or Used (already turned into an account — can't be reused).
Changed your mind?
Delete the invite any time. Deleting one that's still active asks you to confirm first, since the link may already be out there. Deleting a used invite never touches the account it created.
Managing your own account
Anyone can update their info or password from Your Profile.
