Customer covers customer profiles and their credit records. Here's what each part of it does.

  1. 1
    Adding a Customer

    From Customer Home, click Add Customer, fill in the form, and click Create.

    Required: Business Name, Address, Mobile, Cluster, Region, and Classification. Contact Person and Email are optional.

  2. 2
    Finding a Customer

    Click All Customers from the home page to open the customer list. From there:

    • Use the search bar to find by name, mobile, or any visible column.
    • Use the filter dropdown (top-left of the table) to narrow by status or region — Active, Inactive, Lagos, Diaspora, or Outside Lagos.
    • Click a cluster tile on the home page to see all customers in that delivery cluster.
  3. 3
    Viewing a Customer Profile

    In the customer list, click the business name (shown as a link) to open the full profile. It shows contact details, classification, credit status, and activity state.

  4. 4
    Updating a Customer

    Open the customer's profile, click Update in the top navigation, edit the relevant fields, and click Update to save.

  5. 5
    Activating or Disabling a Customer

    Customer profiles are never deleted — they are either Active or Inactive.

    To change status, open the profile and use Update to toggle the Active checkbox. Inactive customers are excluded from active-only list filters.

  6. 6
    Credit Records

    Credit records are managed separately from profiles. From the home page, click Credit Records.

    • To create a credit record for a customer, open their profile — a Create Credit Record link appears if none exists yet.
    • To update a credit limit or mark a customer as blacklisted, open the credit list and use the actions from there.
    • A customer's current credit balance and limit are shown on their profile page.
